# Melnote Privacy Policy

**Effective date:** 17 November 2026  
**Last updated:** 17 November 2026

---

## Overview

Melnote ("we," "us," or "our") is a mobile application for Android and iOS designed to help users manage notes and personal records. Melnote operates on a local-first architecture: all data is stored locally on your device by Melnote, and the application does not utilize user accounts or external servers operated by us.

While Melnote does not transmit your notes or personal records to external servers, please note that personal information (such as names, photos, contact details, journal entries, and relationship data) is still processed and saved locally on your device.

## Information Management

Melnote does not automatically collect or track personal information on external servers. All data is entered entirely at your discretion and stored within the application's private local storage, including:

* **Profile Data:** Names, photos, biographies, gender, and dates of birth.
* **Contact Information:** Phone numbers, email addresses, social links, websites, and postal addresses.
* **Relationships & Organization:** Group categories, labels, and interpersonal connections.
* **Content:** User notes, journal entries, and reminders.
* **Preferences:** Application settings and customization choices.

## Data Collection Limitations

Because Melnote functions without external developer-operated servers, analytics services, or advertising networks, the following information is never automatically collected or transmitted by us:

* Personal identifiers or account credentials.
* Usage analytics, automatic crash reports, diagnostics, or tracking data.
* Device identifiers or advertising IDs.
* Native device contacts or location/GPS data.

Melnote does not sell or share your information with third parties for advertising or analytics.

**Bug reports:** Melnote may keep a local log of errors on your device. This log is only shared if you tap "Report a bug," which opens a draft email in your email app containing the error details and basic device and app information (such as device model, operating system version, and app version). You can review or edit the draft, and nothing is sent unless you send it. We use reports only to diagnose and fix problems.

## Permissions

Melnote requests system permissions strictly at the time features are utilized. Core functionality remains available if permissions are declined.

* **Camera & Media:** Used exclusively for setting profile pictures and attaching images to notes.
* **Notifications:** Used to deliver user-configured reminders.
* **Biometrics:** Used for local application security (App Lock), processed entirely by your operating system.
* **Files:** Used for importing and exporting local backup files.

## Data Security & Storage

* All user records reside within the application's private local storage.
* Passcodes for App Lock are secured via platform-native encryption keystores.
* Screen privacy masking protects information when the app enters the background.
* Melnote does not transmit your notes or personal records to servers operated by us. Certain operating-system and app store services (such as Google Play or Apple) may independently process or back up app data, or collect crash and diagnostic data, depending on your device settings.

## Information Sharing

Melnote does not sell, rent, or share your personal information with third parties for its own commercial, advertising, or analytics purposes. Melnote does not have access to your locally stored records. When you choose to export information or share it through another application, that information may be handled by the recipient or third-party service under its own privacy policy.

## Children's Privacy

Melnote does not knowingly collect personal information from children under the age of 13.

## User Rights & Data Control

Because all data resides locally on your device, you can access, edit, export, and delete your records using the app. You retain direct control over your information without requiring developer intervention.

You can delete your records through the app's available deletion features. Uninstalling the app generally removes its locally stored data, subject to your device's backup and storage settings. Exported backup files must be deleted separately from wherever you saved them.
